# Narrative bible

PLANNED. Navapur is a fictional setting for original crime/action stories about livelihood, power and consequences. No protagonist identity, copied storyline, dialogue or missions are implemented. The depot is a technical fixture, not a story claim.

Future narrative work must represent diverse Indian people without reducing them to stereotypes, establish original characters and local context, and persist mission/consequence state through versioned save adapters. Narrative choices should follow executable interaction capability.

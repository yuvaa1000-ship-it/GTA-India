# World bible

DESIGN CHOICE: Navapur, a fictional Indian port city. Prototype location: Old Port's rectilinear service streets and depot. Warm plaster, muted teal openings, dusty concrete and dark road surfaces establish a blockout palette. Current architecture is generic procedural massing, not a researched representation of a specific real neighborhood.

No real brands, copied maps or copied vehicle silhouettes. Streets repeat on a 64m grid; road direction/parking conventions, authentic signage, regionally researched buildings, believable intersections, accessibility and traffic rules are future BHARAT/LIVING CITY work. Current moving cuboids are debug proxies.

One accessible depot test room at a raised coordinate isolates collision ownership. It is an engineering transition fixture, not a finished interior or mission.
